Cluster App: Fall, Winter and Spring

Three blog seasons that build and then grow a photo-checking chore app on top of my EKS + GitOps home lab. Fall builds the minimum viable product. Winter makes it reliable under pressure. Spring makes it smarter, opens it to more people and measures what it costs. Each season starts by changing the chores through Git and ends by comparing itself to the seasons before.

Before Fall Cleaning #0 (this week)

  • Take the photos now while the leaves are still down: a good and a "wrong" shot for each of the 10 chores. They become the test set for every later part.

Hours include building, debugging and writing each post. Dates assume about 8 hours a week on evenings and weekends, with Thanksgiving and the holidays as lighter weeks.
